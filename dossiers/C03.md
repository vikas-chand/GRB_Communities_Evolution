# C3: GRB Prompt Emission Physics and Polarization

**Defining problem.** What physical mechanism converts the energy of a relativistic GRB outflow into the observed non-thermal gamma-ray spectrum, and what does that mechanism imply about magnetic fields in the ejecta? The community's founding puzzle was the compactness problem — how a source can emit MeV photons without being quenched by pair production [1986ApJ...308L..47G; 1978MNRAS.183..359C]. Its resolution through relativistic fireballs whose radiation is converted to bulk kinetic energy [1990ApJ...365L..55S], rerandomized in external shocks [1993ApJ...405..278M] or internal shocks from an unsteady wind [1994ApJ...430L..93R], set the agenda. Since then the defining question has been whether the prompt emission is synchrotron radiation from shock-accelerated electrons in shock-generated small-scale fields [1994ApJ...432L.107K; 1999ApJ...526..697M], Comptonization in a Poynting-flux-dominated magnetized wind [1994MNRAS.270..480T], or magnetic reconnection in a moderately magnetized jet [2011ApJ...726...90Z] — with gamma-ray and afterglow polarimetry as the decisive observational discriminant [1999ApJ...511..852G; 2003Natur.423..415C; 2019NatAs...3..258Z].

## Populations and events

- Bright BATSE bursts used for spectral-diversity and time-variability population studies (Band function sample; >100 bursts with time-resolved fits) [1993ApJ...413..281B; 1998ApJ...506L..23P; 1993ApJ...408L..81L]
- GRB 021206 — the claimed ~80% polarized burst and its retraction [2003Natur.423..415C; 2004MNRAS.350.1288R; 2004ApJ...613.1088W]
- GRB 041219A — INTEGRAL variable prompt polarization [2009ApJ...695L.208G; 2007A&A...466..895M]
- GRB 100826A — IKAROS/GAP gamma-ray polarization detection [2011ApJ...743L..30Y]
- GRB 080916C — missing photospheric component motivating Poynting-flux-dominated outflows [2011ApJ...726...90Z]
- GRB 160625B — significant, variable polarization of the prompt optical flash [2017Natur.547..425T]
- The five-burst POLAR sample with low polarization degrees and intrapulse angle evolution [2019NatAs...3..258Z]
- The 11-burst (first year) and five-year AstroSat CZTI polarization samples [2019ApJ...884..123C; 2022ApJ...936...12C]
- GRB 160802A — high polarization violating the synchrotron line of death [2018ApJ...862..154C]
- Optical afterglow polarization events GRB 990510, GRB 030329, GRB 090102, GRB 120308A [1999ApJ...523L..33W; 1999A&A...348L...1C; 2003Natur.426..157G; 2009Natur.462..767S; 2013Natur.504..119M]

- IKAROS/GAP gamma-ray polarimeter [2011ApJ...743L..30Y]
- ESO VLT and ground-based optical polarimetry of afterglows [1999ApJ...523L..33W; 2003Natur.426..157G; 2013Natur.504..119M]
- Fermi observations of GRB 080916C and LAT-detected bursts as model constraints [2011ApJ...726...90Z]

## Characteristic methods

- Analytic relativistic fireball/blast-wave dynamics through free-expansion, coasting, and deceleration phases, in comoving and lab frames [1990ApJ...365L..55S; 1993ApJ...415..181M; 1993ApJ...405..278M]
- Compactness/pair-opacity arguments constraining source luminosity and optical depth [1978MNRAS.183..359C; 1986ApJ...308L..47G]
- Internal-shock dissipation modeling of unsteady relativistic winds with fluctuating Lorentz factor [1994ApJ...430L..93R]
- Synchrotron emission spectral modeling in relativistic shocked plasmas, including the nu^{1/3} low-energy segment and analytic afterglow light curves with cooling [1994ApJ...432L.107K; 1999ApJ...511..852G]
- Plasma-instability (relativistic two-stream/Weibel) calculations of magnetic-field generation in collisionless shocks [1999ApJ...526..697M]
- Relativistic MHD and Comptonization modeling of Poynting-flux-dominated winds (Kompaneets equation with relativistic corrections) [1994MNRAS.270..480T]
- Magnetic reconnection and turbulence modeling of magnetized internal collisions (ICMART) [2011ApJ...726...90Z]
- Time-resolved Band-function spectral fitting and population statistics of spectral parameters [1993ApJ...413..281B; 1998ApJ...506L..23P]
- Autocorrelation and skewness-function analysis of burst time histories [1993ApJ...408L..81L]
- Compton-scattering azimuthal-modulation polarimetry with careful null-lightcurve systematics [2003Natur.423..415C; 2004MNRAS.350.1288R; 2019ApJ...884..123C]

## Recurring models

- The relativistic fireball: radiation energy converted to bulk kinetic energy by baryon loading, later rerandomized in shocks [1990ApJ...365L..55S; 1993ApJ...405..278M; 1993ApJ...415..181M]
- Internal shocks in an unsteady wind with Gamma ~ 100 fluctuating by factors of ~2 [1994ApJ...430L..93R]
- The synchrotron shock model with its alpha <= -2/3 low-energy prediction [1994ApJ...432L.107K; 1998ApJ...506L..23P]
- Shock-generated small-scale (skin-depth) magnetic fields via the relativistic two-stream instability, with epsilon_B ~ 1e-5 to 1e-1 of equipartition [1999ApJ...526..697M]
- The Band empirical spectral form: low-energy power law with exponential cutoff plus steeper high-energy power law [1993ApJ...413..281B]
- Poynting-flux-dominated MHD winds from magnetar-strength (>1e14 G) rotators, radiating via Comptonization of Alfven turbulence [1994MNRAS.270..480T]
- ICMART: internal-collision-induced magnetic reconnection and turbulence in a moderately magnetized (1 < sigma < 100) ejecta [2011ApJ...726...90Z]
- Ordered large-scale magnetic fields advected from the central engine as the source of high prompt polarization [2003Natur.423..415C; 2003ApJ...594L..83G; 2003ApJ...597..998L; 2012ApJ...758L...1Y]
- Synchrotron afterglow polarization as a probe of field coherence length in collisionless shocks [1999ApJ...511..852G; 1999ApJ...526..697M]

## Results that shaped it

- Pair-production opacity sets general constraints on rapidly varying gamma-ray sources and motivates either soft standardized spectra or optically thick, distant, relativistically expanding sources — the compactness problem that founded the field [1978MNRAS.183..359C; 1986ApJ...308L..47G]
- Even a small baryon load converts a fireball's radiation into kinetic energy and suppresses the electromagnetic signal, forcing dissipation models [1990ApJ...365L..55S]
- External shocks on ambient matter can rerandomize the fireball's bulk kinetic energy into gamma-rays at cosmological distances [1993ApJ...405..278M]
- Internal shocks from an unsteady outflow with Gamma ~ 100 dissipate outside the photosphere and convert a substantial fraction of outflow energy to non-thermal radiation [1994ApJ...430L..93R]
- BATSE spectra follow the Band form with E_0 peaking below 200 keV and no universal parameter values; a majority are unlikely to be shaped directly by pair processes [1993ApJ...413..281B]
- 23 of >100 bright BATSE bursts violate the synchrotron alpha <= -2/3 'line of death', challenging the simple synchrotron shock model [1998ApJ...506L..23P]
- The relativistic two-stream instability generates skin-depth-scale magnetic fields at 1e-5 to 1e-1 of equipartition in collisionless GRB shocks [1999ApJ...526..697M]
- Afterglow polarization up to ~10% is observable if shock-generated field coherence grows near light speed; detection would confirm the synchrotron afterglow model [1999ApJ...511..852G]
- A Poynting-flux-dominated MHD wind from a >1e14 G rotator reproduces the broken power-law burst spectrum with essentially no free parameters, without any external medium [1994MNRAS.270..480T]
- The claimed near-maximal polarization of GRB 021206 [2003Natur.423..415C] was overturned on re-analysis: the RHESSI signal is consistent with neglected null-lightcurve systematics and carries no polarization constraint [2004MNRAS.350.1288R; 2004ApJ...613.1088W]
- The missing bright photosphere in GRB 080916C argued for Poynting-flux-dominated outflows and the ICMART reconnection model [2011ApJ...726...90Z]
- The first statistically meaningful polarimeter sample (POLAR, 5 bursts) found polarization lower than popular models predicted, with intrapulse polarization-angle evolution [2019NatAs...3..258Z]

## Internal disagreements

- The GRB 021206 polarization controversy: Coburn & Boggs claimed the prompt emission was polarized at the theoretical maximum, implying a uniform large-scale magnetic field that drives the explosion [2003Natur.423..415C]; Rutledge & Fox showed the suitable scattering events were overestimated by a factor of ~10 and the signal is consistent with neglected null-lightcurve systematics, yielding no polarization constraint at all [2004MNRAS.350.1288R], and independent RHESSI limits concurred [2004ApJ...613.1088W] — while theorists had already built interpretations on the original claim [2003ApJ...596L..17G; 2003ApJ...597..998L; 2003ApJ...594L..83G]
- Synchrotron shock model versus the spectral data: the model predicts a low-energy photon index no harder than -2/3 [1994ApJ...432L.107K], but 23 bright BATSE bursts violate this 'line of death' [1998ApJ...506L..23P], and Band et al. found parameter correlations that do not fulfill cosmological-model predictions and no universal spectral values [1993ApJ...413..281B]; later polarimetric work kept the tension alive with a highly polarized line-of-death violator [2018ApJ...862..154C]
- Kinetic-energy-dominated versus Poynting-flux-dominated outflows: the internal-shock picture dissipates bulk kinetic energy of a baryonic wind [1994ApJ...430L..93R; 1993ApJ...405..278M], whereas Thompson's MHD wind generates the spectrum via Comptonization at ~1e10 cm with no external medium at all [1994MNRAS.270..480T], and ICMART argues the missing photosphere of GRB 080916C plus internal-shock efficiency, fast-cooling, and electron-excess problems require a magnetically dominated ejecta dissipating by reconnection [2011ApJ...726...90Z]
- Magnetic field structure in the emission region — ordered versus shock-generated random fields: high or variable prompt polarization detections were read as evidence for ordered large-scale fields advected from the central engine [2003ApJ...594L..83G; 2003ApJ...597..998L; 2011ApJ...743L..30Y; 2019ApJ...882L..10S], while the two-stream-instability picture produces small-scale random fields whose net polarization vanishes under circular symmetry [1999ApJ...526..697M]; the POLAR five-burst sample then found polarization at most at a level lower than popular models predicted, attributing earlier claims partly to systematic uncertainties [2019NatAs...3..258Z], even as AstroSat CZTI reported >=3-sigma polarization signatures in 5 of 11 bright bursts [2019ApJ...884..123C]

## Activity

Founded by 1978-1994 theory (compactness, fireballs, internal/external shocks, synchrotron) [1978MNRAS.183..359C; 1986ApJ...308L..47G; 1994ApJ...430L..93R] and BATSE-era spectroscopy [1993ApJ...413..281B]; a second wave in 1998-2004 around the line of death and the GRB 021206 polarization episode [1998ApJ...506L..23P; 2003Natur.423..415C; 2004MNRAS.350.1288R]; the median year of 2013 reflects the peak of the magnetized-jet and polarimetry era (ICMART 2011, GAP/IKAROS 2011, optical polarimetry 2009-2017) [2011ApJ...726...90Z; 2011ApJ...743L..30Y; 2017Natur.547..425T]. Still active: dedicated polarimeter samples (POLAR, AstroSat CZTI) and time-resolved spectroscopy continued through 2019-2025 [2019NatAs...3..258Z; 2022ApJ...936...12C; 2024ApJ...972..132C; 2025ApJ...987..124D].
