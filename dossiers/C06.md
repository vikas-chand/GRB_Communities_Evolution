# C6: GRB host galaxies and environments

**Defining problem.** What galaxies and interstellar environments produce gamma-ray bursts, and what that reveals both about the progenitors and about cosmic star formation. The community was founded on the proposition that GRB rates track the cosmic star formation history — first computed for delayed compact-binary mergers against the BATSE brightness distribution [1997ApJ...486L..71T], then inverted by the argument that massive-star remnants make GRBs direct SFR tracers, pushing the dimmest bursts to z~6 [1998MNRAS.294L..13W] and promising a dust-free probe of obscured star formation [2000MNRAS.312L..35B] and of the z>5 universe out to z~10-20 [2000ApJ...536....1L]. Its observational program is host identification and characterization: spectroscopic redshifts, star formation rates, extinctions, and metallicities of the galaxies hosting individual bursts [1998ApJ...508L..17D, 2001ApJ...546..672V], burst-to-host offsets as progenitor diagnostics [2002AJ....123.1111B], and the interpretation of optically dark bursts through dust and gas in the burst environment [1998ApJ...493L..27G, 2000ApJ...537..796W, 2009AJ....138.1690P, 2011A&A...526A..30G]. Its late-phase defining question is whether long GRBs are biased tracers of star formation — specifically whether a host-metallicity threshold governs GRB production [2013ApJ...774..119G, 2015A&A...581A.102V, 2016ApJ...817....8P].

## Populations and events

- GRB 970508 — first redshift-constrained burst; its z=0.835 absorber argued not to be the burst site, z~2 favored [1997ApJ...486L..71T]; target of the time-dependent Mg II absorption prediction [1998ApJ...501..467P]
- GRB 980703 — third GRB host redshift ever (z=0.966), SFR ~10 M_sun/yr, then the highest for any GRB galaxy [1998ApJ...508L..17D]
- GRB 970828 — archetypal optically dark burst, no counterpart to R=23.8 [1998ApJ...493L..27G]
- GRB 990123 — prompt optical flash motivating dust-sublimation environment models [2000ApJ...537..796W]
- GRB 990510 (z>=1.619, host V>~28) and GRB 990712 (z=0.4331, brightest known host, H II galaxy) — faint and bright ends of the host population [2001ApJ...546..672V]
- GRB 980329 — spectral cutoff interpreted as H2 absorption at 3.0<z<4.4 [2000ApJ...532..273D]
- GRB 020819 — highest-metallicity long-GRB host environment, 12+log(O/H)=9.0±0.1 [2010ApJ...712L..26L]
- 20 long-GRB host-offset sample, median offset 1.3 kpc [2002AJ....123.1111B]
- Dark bursts as a population: 25-40% of long GRBs, ~22% of them at z>5 [2011A&A...526A..30G]
- Swift/BAT6 complete-sample hosts at z<1 [2015A&A...581A.102V]; SHOALS 119-host legacy sample, 0.03<z<6.3 [2016ApJ...817....8P]; TOUGH optically unbiased host survey [2012ApJ...756..187H]
- Predicted host population: I=21.5-28, peak z~1, ~15% at z>2.5 resembling Lyman-break galaxies [1998A&A...339L...1M]; observed hosts as sub-luminous blue low-mass galaxies at 0.5<z<1.5 [2003A&A...400..499L]

## Instruments and datasets

- BeppoSAX afterglow-era burst localizations [2000ApJ...536....1L]

- GROND multi-channel photometry [2011A&A...526A..30G]
- Palomar 60-inch rapid follow-up of Swift bursts [2009AJ....138.1690P]
- Spitzer deep imaging for rest-frame NIR host luminosities (SHOALS) [2016ApJ...817....8P]
- K-band host photometry compared to deep surveys [2003A&A...400..499L]
- SDSS, TKRS, and UltraVISTA field-galaxy samples as comparison populations [2013ApJ...774..119G, 2015A&A...581A.102V]

## Characteristic methods

- Cosmological rate-density modeling: convolving star formation history (with or without merger delay and a luminosity function) and fitting BATSE number counts [1997ApJ...486L..71T, 1998MNRAS.294L..13W, 1998A&A...339L...1M]
- Emission-line host spectroscopy: redshifts, Balmer-decrement extinction, multi-indicator SFRs, oxygen abundances, and diagnostic-diagram classification [1998ApJ...508L..17D, 2001ApJ...546..672V]
- Afterglow absorption-line spectroscopy of the host ISM, including predicted time-variable absorption from progressive ionization [1998ApJ...501..467P, 2001ApJ...546..672V]
- Astrometric burst-host offset measurement with KS-test comparison to progenitor-model predictions [2002AJ....123.1111B]
- Broadband afterglow SED fitting (optical/NIR + X-ray) to derive rest-frame extinctions and classify dark bursts [2011A&A...526A..30G, 2009AJ....138.1690P]
- Statistical comparison of host metallicity, mass, and SFR distributions against SFR-weighted field-galaxy samples [2013ApJ...774..119G, 2015A&A...581A.102V, 2003A&A...400..499L]
- SED fitting for host stellar masses and rest-frame NIR luminosity functions [2015A&A...581A.102V, 2016ApJ...817....8P]
- Radiative modeling of the circumburst medium: dust sublimation by the UV flash and H2 pumping/fluorescence in molecular clouds [2000ApJ...537..796W, 2000ApJ...532..273D]

## Recurring models

- GRB rate proportional to the cosmic star formation rate, the community's master hypothesis [1998MNRAS.294L..13W, 1998A&A...339L...1M, 2000MNRAS.312L..35B]
- Massive-star/collapsar progenitors placing bursts inside star-forming regions [2000ApJ...536....1L, 2002AJ....123.1111B, 2003A&A...400..499L]
- Compact-binary merger progenitors with delay times — the founding alternative, later disfavored for long bursts by offsets [1997ApJ...486L..71T, 2002AJ....123.1111B]
- Metallicity-threshold models for long-GRB production (Z_th = 0.3-0.5 Z_sun to ~solar) [2015A&A...581A.102V, 2016ApJ...817....8P, 2013ApJ...774..119G]
- Dust extinction local to the burst as the explanation for dark bursts [2009AJ....138.1690P, 2011A&A...526A..30G, 2003A&A...400..499L]
- Circumburst radiative processing: dust sublimation out to ~10 pc by the prompt UV flash, time-dependent absorption from ionization of a <~100 pc cloud, H2 absorption/fluorescence in molecular clouds [2000ApJ...537..796W, 1998ApJ...501..467P, 2000ApJ...532..273D]
- GRBs as high-redshift probes: absorption spectroscopy of metallicity evolution, Ly-alpha forest, and reionization via the Gunn-Peterson test [2000ApJ...536....1L]

## Results that shaped it

- SFR-tracking rate models push the dimmest BATSE bursts to z~6, implying peak luminosities ~20x higher and rates ~150x lower than non-evolving fits — so GRBs are rarer than BNS mergers or beamed [1998MNRAS.294L..13W]
- Rate-evolution ~(1+z)^2-2.5 from the delayed BNS-merger + star-formation-history calculation against BATSE counts [1997ApJ...486L..71T]
- GRB 980703 host: unambiguous z=0.966 and SFR>7 M_sun/yr, early direct evidence tying GRBs to massive star formation [1998ApJ...508L..17D]
- Median burst-host offset of 1.3 kpc tracking host UV light; delayed merging remnants ruled out at the 2x10^-3 level for long GRBs [2002AJ....123.1111B]
- Prediction of the host population from SFR-tracking models: hosts peak at z~1, I=21.5-28, ~15% at z>2.5 like Lyman-break galaxies [1998A&A...339L...1M]
- GRB hosts are sub-luminous, blue, low-mass galaxies, hinting at a metal-poor preference consistent with collapsars [2003A&A...400..499L]
- GROND census: 25-40% of long GRBs are dark, mostly from moderate intrinsic extinction, with ~22% of dark bursts at z>5 [2011A&A...526A..30G]; dust is the dominant cause of darkness and dark-burst hosts have normal optical colors [2009AJ....138.1690P]
- Three quarters of long-GRB hosts lie below 12+log(O/H)=8.6 versus <10% of local star formation — a metal aversion intrinsic to the progenitors [2013ApJ...774..119G]
- BAT6 complete sample: long GRBs avoid massive galaxies at z<1 and are biased SFR tracers, requiring Z_th=0.3-0.5 Z_sun [2015A&A...581A.102V]
- SHOALS: GRB production is suppressed only above ~solar metallicity, a threshold high enough to favor binary progenitors [2016ApJ...817....8P]
- GRBs established as probes of z>5: detectable to z~10-20, tracing metallicity evolution and reionization [2000ApJ...536....1L]
- Dust sublimation by the prompt flash reconciles unreddened afterglows with star-forming-region birthplaces [2000ApJ...537..796W]

## Internal disagreements

- Progenitor identity read from hosts: the community founder computed GRB rates assuming delayed binary neutron star mergers [1997ApJ...486L..71T], while the offset distribution of 20 long GRBs later ruled out delayed merging remnants at the 2x10^-3 level in favor of collapsar-like progenitors tracing host UV light [2002AJ....123.1111B]; already in 1998 the SFR-tracking flux fits implied GRBs are far rarer than BNS mergers unless strongly beamed [1998MNRAS.294L..13W].
- How strong is the metallicity bias: Graham & Fruchter find three quarters of long-GRB hosts below 12+log(O/H)=8.6 and call sub-half-solar metallicity a fundamental requirement [2013ApJ...774..119G]; the BAT6 sample requires a threshold of 0.3-0.5 Z_sun and excludes both no-threshold and extreme 0.1 Z_sun models [2015A&A...581A.102V]; SHOALS instead places the suppression threshold near solar metallicity — much higher, and favoring binary progenitors over single-star models [2016ApJ...817....8P]; while GRB 020819's host environment at 12+log(O/H)=9.0 stands as a direct high-metallicity counterexample [2010ApJ...712L..26L].
- Are GRBs unbiased SFR tracers at all: the founding program treated the burst redshift distribution as a dust-free tracer of massive star formation [2000MNRAS.312L..35B, 1998MNRAS.294L..13W], but the BAT6 host analysis concludes long GRBs are not unbiased tracers at z<1 and only track SFR directly from z~4 [2015A&A...581A.102V]; SHOALS confines the bias to z<1.5 with minor impact at higher redshift [2016ApJ...817....8P].
- What makes dark bursts dark: H2 absorption in molecular clouds was proposed to explain the GRB 980329 cutoff at inferred 3.0<z<4.4 without dust [2000ApJ...532..273D], and dust sublimation by the prompt flash was argued to clear dust so that unreddened fireballs stay consistent with star-forming regions [2000ApJ...537..796W]; the Swift-era resolution attributes darkness dominantly to dust extinction local to the progenitor [2009AJ....138.1690P], with GROND quantifying moderate intrinsic extinction plus a ~22% z>5 tail [2011A&A...526A..30G].

## Activity

Founded 1997-2000 in the afterglow era (Totani 1997, Wijers 1998, the first host spectroscopy in 1998 [1998ApJ...508L..17D]), with a first observational consolidation in 2001-2003 (offsets [2002AJ....123.1111B], host demographics [2003A&A...400..499L]). Peak activity in the Swift era, consistent with the community median year of 2012: the dark-burst census (2009-2011 [2009AJ....138.1690P, 2011A&A...526A..30G]) and the complete-sample host surveys TOUGH 2012 [2012ApJ...756..187H], BAT6 2015-2019 [2015A&A...581A.102V, 2019A&A...623A..26P], and SHOALS 2016 [2016ApJ...817....8P]. Currently mature and tapering: the big statistical questions (metallicity threshold, tracer bias) were largely framed and bounded by 2016-2019, and the corpus shows its host-identification toolkit being inherited by fast-radio-burst host studies from 2016 onward [2017ApJ...834L...7T].
