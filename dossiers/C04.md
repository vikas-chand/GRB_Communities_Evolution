# C4: GRB multimessenger high-energy astrophysics

**Defining problem.** Whether gamma-ray bursts are the accelerators of the highest-energy particles in the universe — and how to test that with GeV photons, ultra-high-energy cosmic rays, and high-energy neutrinos. The community was seeded by EGRET's detection of GeV photons from bright BATSE bursts, including an 18 GeV photon arriving ~90 minutes after GRB 940217 [1994Natur.372..652H] and a GeV power-law tail from GRB 930131 [1994ApJ...422L..63S; 1994ApJ...422L..59K], and by the theoretical proposals that GRB blast waves naturally produce delayed GeV emission [1994MNRAS.269L..41M] and that GRB internal shocks Fermi-accelerate protons to ~10^20 eV, making GRBs the source of ultra-high-energy cosmic rays [1995PhRvL..75..386W; 1995ApJ...453..883V]. The corollary prediction — that photomeson production in fireballs converts >=10% of the energy into ~10^14 eV neutrinos, detectable at tens of events per year by a km^2 detector [1997PhRvL..78.2292W] — set the agenda that IceCube spent two decades testing [2015ApJ...805L...5A].

## Populations and events

- GRB 930131, the 'superbowl burst': most intense BATSE GRB, GeV photons and five-decade spectrum [1994ApJ...422L..63S; 1994ApJ...422L..59K]
- GRB 940217: 90-minute high-energy emission including an 18 GeV photon [1994Natur.372..652H]
- GRB 940217's delayed 25 GeV photons as target of hadronic modeling [1994ApJ...432L..27K]
- Ultra-high-energy cosmic rays above 10^20 eV, including the Bird et al. (Fly's Eye) event [1995PhRvL..75..386W; 1995ApJ...453..883V]
- 506 satellite-detected GRBs stacked in the four-year IceCube prompt-neutrino search [2015ApJ...805L...5A]
- 215 bursts during IceCube 40- and 59-string configurations [2012ApJ...752...29H]
- GW170817 / binary neutron star merger, searched for high-energy neutrinos by ANTARES, IceCube, and Auger [2017ApJ...850L..35A]
- Low-luminosity and choked-jet GRBs as hidden neutrino source populations [2016PhRvD..93h3003S; 2013PhRvL.111l1102M]

## Instruments and datasets

- EGRET on the Compton Gamma-Ray Observatory, 30 MeV to >10 GeV [1988SSRv...49...69K; 1994ApJ...422L..63S]
- BATSE, including 1-ms temporal resolution spectroscopy [1994ApJ...422L..59K]
- Interplanetary Network triangulation [1994ApJ...422L..63S]
- Fermi Large Area Telescope, 20 MeV to >300 GeV, 2.4 sr field of view [2009ApJ...697.1071A]
- IceCube neutrino observatory (40-string, 59-string, and full-detector configurations) [2012ApJ...752...29H; 2015ApJ...805L...5A]
- ANTARES and the Pierre Auger Observatory in joint multimessenger searches [2017ApJ...850L..35A]
- Fly's Eye / cosmic-ray air-shower measurements above 10^20 eV [1995ApJ...453..883V]

## Characteristic methods

- Photomeson (p-gamma) production and hadronic-cascade flux calculations, analytic and numerical [1997PhRvL..78.2292W; 1998PhRvD..58l3005R; 2012PhRvL.108w1101H]
- Fermi acceleration arguments in relativistic winds and shocks to derive maximum proton energies [1995PhRvL..75..386W; 1995ApJ...453..883V]
- Model-independent upper bounds on diffuse neutrino intensity from the observed cosmic-ray flux (the Waxman-Bahcall bound) [1998PhRvD..59b3002W]
- Blast-wave / reverse-shock relativistic hydrodynamics for delayed GeV emission [1994MNRAS.269L..41M]
- Stacked, temporally coincident neutrino searches against atmospheric background over GRB catalogs [2015ApJ...805L...5A; 2012ApJ...752...29H]
- Secondary-particle (pion, muon, kaon) cooling treatments that set neutrino spectral cutoffs [1998PhRvD..58l3005R; 2012PhRvL.108w1101H]
- Pair-conversion telescope imaging and power-law spectral fitting of GeV photons [2009ApJ...697.1071A; 1994ApJ...422L..63S]

## Recurring models

- The dissipative ultrarelativistic wind / fireball with internal shocks, Lorentz factor ~100 [1994MNRAS.269L..41M; 1997PhRvL..78.2292W]
- GRBs as sources of ultra-high-energy cosmic rays via Fermi acceleration of protons to ~10^20 eV [1995PhRvL..75..386W; 1995ApJ...453..883V]
- The fireball photomeson neutrino model: >=10% of fireball energy into ~10^14 eV neutrinos [1997PhRvL..78.2292W]
- External-shock blast wave and reverse shock producing delayed GeV emission [1994MNRAS.269L..41M]
- Hadronic pi^0-decay models for delayed hard photons (nucleon collisions with a dense cloud, n ~ 2e11 cm^-3) [1994ApJ...432L..27K]
- The internal-shock benchmark parameterization (Gamma ~ 10^2.5, t_v ~ 0.01 s, baryon loading eta_p ~ 10) used to confront IceCube limits [2012ApJ...752...29H]
- Choked jets and low-luminosity GRBs as hidden (gamma-ray-dark) neutrino sources [2013PhRvL.111l1102M; 2016PhRvD..93h3003S]

## Results that shaped it

- EGRET detection of GeV photons from GRB 930131: 16 photons above 30 MeV with photon index -2.0 +/- 0.4, two at ~1 GeV [1994ApJ...422L..63S]
- Detection of an 18 GeV photon in a 90-minute burst (GRB 940217), proving GRB emission extends to very high energies and late times [1994Natur.372..652H]
- The joint BATSE+EGRET spectrum of GRB 930131 covering five energy decades up to the GeV range [1994ApJ...422L..59K]
- Prediction of delayed GeV emission from the blast wave and reverse shock of a decelerating relativistic wind [1994MNRAS.269L..41M]
- The GRB-UHECR connection: protons Fermi-accelerated to ~10^20 eV in GRB winds reproduce the observed cosmic-ray flux above 10^20 eV [1995PhRvL..75..386W; 1995ApJ...453..883V]
- The Waxman-Bahcall prediction of ~10^14 eV neutrino bursts detectable at tens of events per year by a km^2 detector [1997PhRvL..78.2292W]
- The model-independent Waxman-Bahcall upper bound E^2 Phi < 2e-8 GeV cm^-2 s^-1 sr^-1 on neutrinos from cosmic-ray sources [1998PhRvD..59b3002W]
- Secondary pion/muon cooling limits that rule out major GRB/blazar neutrino contributions above 10^19 eV [1998PhRvD..58l3005R]
- Launch of Fermi-LAT, extending GRB high-energy coverage to >300 GeV with rapid transient notification [2009ApJ...697.1071A]
- IceCube's four-year null result: one background-compatible event in 506 bursts, limiting prompt GRB emission to <~1% of the astrophysical neutrino flux [2015ApJ...805L...5A]

## Internal disagreements

- Predicted GRB neutrino flux normalization: the founding fireball model predicted a km^2 detector would see at least several tens of events per year [1997PhRvL..78.2292W], but revised numerical calculations with full energy dependencies found the analytical models overestimate the flux by about one order of magnitude [2012PhRvL.108w1101H], and an independent revised analytic calculation likewise obtained a flux one order of magnitude below the IceCube Collaboration's own benchmark [2012ApJ...752...29H] — so whether IceCube's nondetection killed the model or only the early flux arithmetic was disputed
- GRBs as UHECR sources: proposed as the origin of cosmic rays above 10^20 eV [1995PhRvL..75..386W; 1995ApJ...453..883V], but photohadronic cascade limits ruled out major GRB contributions above 10^19 eV under standard energetic constraints, finding joint UHECR-plus-neutrino production possible only under extreme conditions [1998PhRvD..58l3005R], and IceCube's stacked limits later disfavored much of the parameter space of the latest UHECR-from-GRB models [2015ApJ...805L...5A]
- Origin of delayed GeV photons: leptonic external blast-wave / reverse-shock emission from the decelerating wind [1994MNRAS.269L..41M] versus hadronic pi^0 production by relativistic nucleons striking a dense (~2e11 cm^-3) remnant cloud around the merger [1994ApJ...432L..27K]
- Where the observed astrophysical neutrinos come from, given classical GRBs contribute <~1% [2015ApJ...805L...5A]: choked jets and low-luminosity GRBs invisible in gamma rays were advanced as the hidden source population [2013PhRvL.111l1102M; 2016PhRvD..93h3003S]

## Activity

Founded in 1994-1998 by the EGRET GeV detections [1994Natur.372..652H; 1994ApJ...422L..63S] and the UHECR/neutrino predictions [1995PhRvL..75..386W; 1997PhRvL..78.2292W]; most active around the median year 2012, when Fermi-LAT [2009ApJ...697.1071A] and IceCube's deepening nondetections [2012ApJ...752...29H; 2012PhRvL.108w1101H; 2015ApJ...805L...5A] forced model revision. Still alive in the multimessenger era: joint GW170817 neutrino searches in 2017 [2017ApJ...850L..35A], hidden-source models [2016PhRvD..93h3003S], and lepto-hadronic modeling of energetic GRBs as recently as 2023 [2023ApJ...950...28R].
