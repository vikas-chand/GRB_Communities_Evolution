# C13: Quark-Star GRB Central Engines

**Defining problem.** Whether the deconfinement of quark matter in compact stars — collisions of strange stars [1991ApJ...375..209H], accretion- or spin-down-induced conversion of neutron stars to strange/quark stars [1996PhRvL..77.1210C; 1996ApJ...462L..63M; 2000ApJ...530L..69B], or delayed nucleation of a quark core in a metastable hadronic star [2003ApJ...586.1250B; 2004ApJ...614..314B] — can supply the energy (10^50–10^53 erg), the low baryon loading, and the event rate required to power cosmological gamma-ray bursts, and how such events would connect to supernovae and to observable compact-star populations [2000A&A...357..543W; 2016PhRvD..94h3010L].

## Populations and events

- Cosmological GRB population and its rate (~10^-6/yr/galaxy) matched to quark-star formation rates [1996ApJ...462L..63M; 1996PhRvL..77.1210C]
- BATSE weak-burst source counts used to constrain strange-star origins [1991ApJ...375..209H; 1994A&A...286L..17K]
- The 5 March 1979 gamma-ray transient, the community's founding event [1986PhRvL..57.2088A; 1987PhRvL..58.2152A]
- GRB 990705 and GRB 011211, the delayed SN-GRB cases explained by delayed quark-matter collapse [2003ApJ...586.1250B; 2002A&A...390L..39O]
- SN 1998bw and the supernova/GRB connection [2000A&A...357..543W]
- Neutron stars in low-mass X-ray binaries as conversion progenitors [1996PhRvL..77.1210C]
- Short GRBs with internal X-ray plateaus, ~22% supramassive-remnant fraction from Swift data [2016PhRvD..94h3010L]
- The bimodal short/long GRB duration distribution, mapped to two engine regimes (~2 s and ~81 s) [2002A&A...387..725O]

## Instruments and datasets

- BATSE/Compton GRO angular distribution and source-count statistics of weak bursts [1991ApJ...375..209H; 1994A&A...286L..17K]
- Swift X-ray afterglow light curves of short GRBs (internal plateaus and break times) [2016PhRvD..94h3010L]
- X-ray spectra of compact sources used for mass-radius extraction [2004ApJ...614..314B]
- Gravitational-wave temporal signatures proposed to discriminate quark-star formation from NS mergers [1996ApJ...462L..63M]

## Characteristic methods

- Analytic energy-budget calculations of the binding-energy release in NS-to-strange-star conversion using competing equations of state [2000ApJ...530L..69B; 1996PhRvL..77.1210C]
- Nucleation theory with finite-size/surface-tension effects to compute quark-matter drop formation times and stellar metastability lifetimes [2003ApJ...586.1250B; 2004ApJ...614..314B]
- Fireball dynamics and baryon-loading estimates exploiting the bare strange-star surface (Lorentz factors 100 to >5x10^3) [1996PhRvL..77.1210C; 2000A&A...357..543W]
- 1D numerical hydrodynamic simulation of phase-transition-induced collapse with realistic neutrino physics and fine surface zoning [1998ApJ...501..780F]
- Order-of-magnitude burst-rate estimation from stellar evolution and spin-down timescales [1996ApJ...462L..63M; 1991ApJ...375..209H]
- Monte Carlo population synthesis of SGRB observables over NS and QS equation-of-state families [2016PhRvD..94h3010L]
- Source-count statistics combined with strangelet-contamination arguments to exclude models [1994A&A...286L..17K]

## Recurring models

- Bodmer-Witten hypothesis that strange quark matter is the absolutely stable ground state of matter, prerequisite of the whole community [1994A&A...286L..17K; 1996PhRvL..77.1210C]
- Strange-star collision model for extragalactic GRBs [1991ApJ...375..209H]
- Accretion-induced NS-to-strange-star conversion in LMXBs producing a clean fireball [1996PhRvL..77.1210C; 2000ApJ...530L..69B]
- Spin-down-triggered quark-core phase transition / super-giant glitch [1996ApJ...462L..63M; 2000A&A...357..543W]
- Delayed collapse of a metastable hadronic star via quark-matter nucleation, explaining SN-GRB time delays (the two-step / two-families scenario) [2003ApJ...586.1250B; 2004ApJ...614..314B; 2016EPJA...52...41D]
- Quark-Nova: core collapse to a quark star with envelope ejection, up to 10^53 erg [2002A&A...390L..39O]
- Color-superconducting (2SC / CFL) quark-star surfaces as photon-fireball generators [2002A&A...387..725O]
- Differentially rotating strange stars becoming millisecond pulsars after the burst [1998PhRvL..81.4301D]
- Supramassive fast-rotating quark stars as short-GRB postmerger remnants [2016PhRvD..94h3010L]

## Results that shaped it

- Strange-star collisions release ~10^50 erg in gamma rays over 0.2 s, detectable to ~1 Gpc — the founding quantitative claim [1991ApJ...375..209H]
- Alcock, Farhi & Olinto's strange-star model for the 5 March 1979 transient seeded the field [1986PhRvL..57.2088A; 1987PhRvL..58.2152A]
- NS-to-strange-star conversion in LMXBs yields GRB-scale energy with Lorentz factors >5x10^3 from low baryon contamination [1996PhRvL..77.1210C]
- Kluzniak's exclusion: strangelet contamination plus BATSE source counts rule out strange-star disruption as the GRB source [1994A&A...286L..17K]
- Fryer & Woosley's simulations: phase-transition collapse gives <10^46 erg in material with Gamma>40 and 0.01 Msun of baryon-rich ejecta — far too dirty and weak for GRBs [1998ApJ...501..780F]
- Bombaci & Datta: conversion liberates (1-4)x10^53 erg, an order of magnitude above earlier estimates, reviving the engine's energetics [2000ApJ...530L..69B]
- Surface tension delays quark-matter nucleation, letting the collapse occur days-to-years after the supernova and explaining GRB 990705 and GRB 011211 [2003ApJ...586.1250B]
- Quark deconfinement with finite-size effects makes massive NSs metastable, tying the limiting mass of compact stars to a two-step SN-then-GRB process [2004ApJ...614..314B]
- Quark-star equations of state reproduce the narrow break-time distribution of internal X-ray plateaus in Swift short GRBs better than NS EoSs [2016PhRvD..94h3010L]

## Internal disagreements

- Can strange stars power GRBs at all? Haensel et al. proposed strange-star collisions as the least speculative extragalactic GRB engine [1991ApJ...375..209H], and Cheng & Dai argued conversion events satisfy all cosmological GRB requirements [1996PhRvL..77.1210C]; Kluzniak countered that strangelet release from any strange-star disruption, combined with BATSE source-count statistics, excludes strange stars as sources of the observed bursts [1994A&A...286L..17K].
- Clean versus dirty fireballs: analytic models claimed the bare strange-star surface yields very low baryon contamination with Lorentz factors of ~100 to >5x10^3 [1996PhRvL..77.1210C; 2000A&A...357..543W], but Fryer & Woosley's numerical collapse simulations found the ejecta are far too baryon-rich (~0.01 Msun) with <10^46 erg at Gamma>40 — too little to explain GRBs [1998ApJ...501..780F].
- Conversion energy budget: early estimates put the phase-transition energy at ~10^52 erg [1996ApJ...462L..63M], while Bombaci & Datta computed (1-4)x10^53 erg and explicitly flagged it as an order of magnitude larger than previous estimates [2000ApJ...530L..69B].
- Where the fireball is made: Ouyed & Sannino generate the fireball from unstable 2SC particles at an existing quark-star surface, an approach they present as fundamentally different from models where the fireball is generated during the collapse or conversion itself [2002A&A...387..725O vs 1996PhRvL..77.1210C; 2000ApJ...530L..69B].

## Activity

Founded by the 1986-1987 strange-star interpretation of the 5 March 1979 transient [1986PhRvL..57.2088A; 1987PhRvL..58.2152A] and Haensel's 1991 collision model [1991ApJ...375..209H], the community was most active from the mid-1990s BATSE era through the mid-2000s (conversion, quark-nova, and delayed-collapse models, 1996-2004; median year 2006). It has since narrowed but persists: the multimessenger era repurposed the same physics for short-GRB remnants and the two-families scenario, with active work in 2016 on supramassive quark stars from Swift internal plateaus [2016PhRvD..94h3010L] and hadronic-to-quark transitions in explosive phenomena [2016EPJA...52...41D].
