# C11: Indirect Dark Matter Detection

**Defining problem.** Whether dark matter can be detected indirectly through the gamma-ray and positron-annihilation photons its particle annihilations produce — in the Galactic center, the Galactic bulge, dwarf spheroidals, and the cosmological extragalactic background — and, critically, whether observed high-energy anomalies (the EGRET Galactic-center source [2004APh....21..267C], the HESS TeV signal [2005PhRvL..94m1301B], the INTEGRAL/SPI 511 keV bulge line [2004PhRvL..92j1301B], and the Fermi 1-3 GeV Galactic-center excess [2014PhRvL.113o1302F]) are annihilating dark matter or conventional astrophysics. The community sits inside the GRB corpus because GRBs/hypernovae in the Galactic center are the leading astrophysical competitor for the positron budget behind the 511 keV line [2004ApJ...602L..17C, 2005A&A...432..889P, 2006PhLB..636...20B].

## Populations and events

- The EGRET gamma-ray source at the Galactic center, whose spectrum is compatible with WIMP annihilation [2004APh....21..267C]
- The INTEGRAL/SPI 511 keV positron-annihilation line from the Galactic bulge, extended, bulge-shaped, with no high-energy or 26Al counterpart [2004PhRvL..92j1301B, 2004ApJ...602L..17C]
- The HESS TeV gamma-ray signal from the Galactic-center direction [2005PhRvL..94m1301B]
- The Fermi 1-3 GeV Galactic-center excess [2014PhRvL.113o1302F]
- Milky Way dwarf spheroidal satellites (Sagittarius, Draco, the combined 15-dSph sample) as clean dark-matter targets [2004PhRvL..93p1302H, 2015PhRvL.115w1301A]
- GRB-associated Type Ic hypernovae (SN 2003dh-like) in the Galactic center as candidate positron injectors [2004ApJ...602L..17C]
- The neutron-star population of the Galactic bulge, used to bound Kaluza-Klein graviton decay [2004PhRvL..92k1102C]
- Unresolved blazars ('grazars') as the astrophysical background to the extragalactic gamma-ray signal [1994ApJ...430L..21S]

## Instruments and datasets

- INTEGRAL/SPI 511 keV spectroscopy and spatial mapping of the Galactic bulge [2004PhRvL..92j1301B, 2004ApJ...602L..17C, 2009PhRvL.103c1301L]
- EGRET catalog data: the Galactic-center source and blazar/diffuse-background measurements [2004APh....21..267C, 1994ApJ...430L..21S, 2005PhRvL..94q1301B]
- COMPTEL diffuse gamma-ray constraints [2005PhRvL..94q1301B]
- HESS atmospheric Cherenkov TeV observations of the Galactic center [2005PhRvL..94m1301B]
- GLAST/Fermi-LAT, first as the anticipated discriminator [2002PhRvD..66l3502U, 2004APh....21..267C], then delivering six-year Pass8 dwarf-spheroidal limits [2015PhRvL.115w1301A] and the GeV Galactic-center excess [2014PhRvL.113o1302F]

## Characteristic methods

- Analytical particle-physics computation of annihilation spectra, including full one-loop calculations of line channels (chi chi -> Z gamma, 2 gamma) in the MSSM [1998PhRvD..57.1962U] and internal-bremsstrahlung radiative corrections [2005PhRvL..94m1301B, 2005PhRvL..94q1301B]
- Dark-matter halo-profile modeling (NFW, Kravtsov, minicusps, black-hole spikes) to set the annihilation flux normalization [1998PhRvD..57.1962U, 2004PhRvL..93f1302G, 2014PhRvL.113o1302F]
- N-body-simulation-calibrated structure-formation modeling of the cosmological annihilation signal [2002PhRvD..66l3502U]
- Relic-density (thermal freeze-out) consistency checks on proposed candidates [2004PhRvL..92j1301B, 2005PhRvL..94m1301B]
- Rate/budget accounting: comparing positron injection rates from candidate sources (hypernovae, GRB pair leakage, SNe Ia) against the annihilation rate implied by the 511 keV flux [2004ApJ...602L..17C, 2006PhLB..636...20B, 2005A&A...432..889P]
- Spectral and angular signature fitting to telescope data, and stacked/population upper limits (15 combined dwarfs) [2004APh....21..267C, 2015PhRvL.115w1301A]

## Recurring models

- Supersymmetric neutralino (LSP) WIMP dark matter, including mSUGRA and pure-Higgsino limits [2002PhRvD..66l3502U, 1998PhRvD..57.1962U, 2004APh....21..267C]
- Light (1-100 MeV) annihilating dark matter as the source of Galactic-bulge positrons [2004PhRvL..92j1301B, 2004PhRvL..93p1302H]
- Kaluza-Klein dark matter and large-extra-dimension gravitons from TeV-scale extra dimensions [2005PhRvL..94m1301B, 2004PhRvL..92k1102C]
- Characteristic annihilation spectral signatures: pi0 continuum with a sharp cutoff at the WIMP mass and monochromatic lines redshift-smeared into asymmetric bumps [2002PhRvD..66l3502U]; flat internal-bremsstrahlung spectra with abrupt mass cutoffs [2005PhRvL..94m1301B]
- Inner-halo density enhancements and their dynamics: the r^-3/2 stellar-scattering minicusp around Sgr A* [2004PhRvL..93f1302G] and adiabatic black-hole spikes [2014PhRvL.113o1302F]
- Astrophysical positron-source alternatives: bipolar hypernova 56Ni ejection with polar positron escape [2004ApJ...602L..17C], GRB pair leakage and photon pair-production in molecular clouds [2006PhLB..636...20B], CMZ mini-starburst GRBs with bulge-wide positron diffusion [2005A&A...432..889P], and nucleosynthetic beta+ decay with extensive propagation [2009PhRvL.103c1301L]

## Results that shaped it

- Srednicki, Theisen & Silk showed halo WIMP annihilation into quarkonium plus a monochromatic photon could produce observable sharp gamma-ray peaks — the founding line-signature idea [1986PhRvL..56..263S]
- Full one-loop MSSM calculation of neutralino annihilation to Z gamma established the two-line (2gamma + Zgamma) diagnostic of the WIMP's nature [1998PhRvD..57.1962U]
- The community founder computed the cosmological WIMP annihilation contribution to the extragalactic gamma-ray background, with pi0 cutoff and redshift-smeared line bumps detectable by GLAST [2002PhRvD..66l3502U], against the blazar background quantified from EGRET [1994ApJ...430L..21S]
- Boehm et al. proposed the INTEGRAL 511 keV bulge emission as 1-100 MeV dark matter annihilation consistent with the relic density — the result that ignited the 2004-2005 burst [2004PhRvL..92j1301B]
- Cassé et al. countered that ~0.02 SN 2003dh-like GRB/hypernovae per century in the Galactic center suffice for the positron flux, tying the problem to GRB astrophysics [2004ApJ...602L..17C]
- Internal bremsstrahlung from 'invisible' annihilation violates COMPTEL/EGRET unless the dark matter mass is below ~20 MeV, sharply squeezing the MeV window [2005PhRvL..94q1301B]
- The EGRET Galactic-center source shown compatible with WIMP annihilation, with GLAST as arbiter [2004APh....21..267C]; the HESS TeV signal fit by ~1-10 TeV Kaluza-Klein dark matter [2005PhRvL..94m1301B]
- Stellar scattering off the Sgr A* cluster drives the inner-parsec profile to a universal r^-3/2 minicusp, fixing the Galactic-center flux prediction [2004PhRvL..93f1302G]
- Six years of Fermi-LAT Pass8 data on 15 dwarf spheroidals yielded no detection and pushed limits below the thermal relic cross section for masses below ~100 GeV [2015PhRvL.115w1301A]

## Internal disagreements

- The 1986 quarkonium-line rate dispute: Rich showed the Srednicki-Theisen-Silk calculation [1986PhRvL..56..263S] 'seriously overestimates' the annihilation-to-quarkonium rate by an order of magnitude or more by neglecting the quarkonium bound-state structure [1986PhRvL..56.2128R]
- Origin of the 511 keV bulge positrons — the community's central fight: MeV dark matter annihilation [2004PhRvL..92j1301B, 2004PhRvL..93p1302H] versus GRB/hypernova astrophysics [2004ApJ...602L..17C, 2005A&A...432..889P]; the GRB-leakage variant itself found only 'marginally consistent', needing above-average GRB rates or molecular-cloud optical depths [2006PhLB..636...20B]
- Whether MeV dark matter survives its own gamma rays: internal bremsstrahlung violates COMPTEL/EGRET unless m < ~20 MeV [2005PhRvL..94q1301B], directly constraining the 1-100 MeV window claimed by [2004PhRvL..92j1301B]
- Whether the 511 keV morphology is dark-matter evidence at all: Lingenfelter et al. argued that with realistic positron propagation, standard nucleosynthetic beta+ sources fully reproduce the spatial distribution and ~97% positronium fraction, 'leaving no new signal for dark matter to explain' [2009PhRvL.103c1301L] — against the dark-matter reading of the same map [2004PhRvL..92j1301B]
- The black-hole-spike tension: if the Fermi GeV excess is dark matter, a canonical adiabatic spike around Sgr A* would outshine it 35-fold and is disfavored by the data, contradicting the standard spike-formation picture unless Galactic history was non-adiabatic [2014PhRvL.113o1302F] (compare the milder minicusp of [2004PhRvL..93f1302G])

## Activity

Seeded in 1986 with the quarkonium gamma-ray line idea [1986PhRvL..56..263S, 1986PhRvL..56.2128R] and built through 1990s loop calculations [1998PhRvD..57.1962U] and the 2002 cosmological-signal paper [2002PhRvD..66l3502U], the community exploded in 2004-2005 around the INTEGRAL 511 keV line, the EGRET Galactic-center source, and the HESS TeV signal (most of the locally influential papers date to those two years; median year 2007). A second, Fermi-era wave ran through 2009-2015 on the GeV Galactic-center excess and dwarf-spheroidal limits [2014PhRvL.113o1302F, 2015PhRvL.115w1301A]. Within this GRB corpus it is now largely dormant: no influential member paper postdates 2015, the MeV-dark-matter reading of the 511 keV line having been squeezed by gamma-ray constraints [2005PhRvL..94q1301B] and propagation arguments [2009PhRvL.103c1301L], and the WIMP program left holding sub-thermal-relic upper limits [2015PhRvL.115w1301A].
