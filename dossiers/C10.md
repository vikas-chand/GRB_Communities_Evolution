# C10: Fireshell / binary-driven hypernova program

**Defining problem.** To explain the entire GRB phenomenon — energetics, light-curve structure, spectra, the GRB-supernova connection, and burst classification — from first-principles relativistic field theory and binary stellar evolution, rather than from the standard fireball/internal-shock framework. The founding problem is whether vacuum polarization around a charged ("electromagnetic") black hole can power GRBs: the dyadosphere, the region where the field exceeds the Heisenberg-Euler critical value for e+e- pair creation, was proposed as the GRB energy source [1998A&A...338L..87P], with the resulting pair-electromagnetic (PEM) pulse computed via general-relativistic hydrodynamics to yield Lorentz factors up to 6x10^3 and photon energies of 10^52-10^54 erg consistent with observed bursts [1999A&A...350..334R, 1999A&AS..138..511R]. The later phase of the problem became the progenitor question: how a tight CO-core + neutron-star binary produces a GRB when the supernova of the core drives hypercritical accretion onto the companion NS, collapsing it to a black hole — the induced-gravitational-collapse / binary-driven-hypernova (BdHN) paradigm [2012ApJ...758L...7R, 2014ApJ...793L..36F].

## Populations and events

- GRB 991216 — the community's original prototype, used to fix the two free parameters of the EMBH model and to define the injector/beam-target burst-structure paradigm [2001ApJ...555L.113R, 2001ApJ...555L.107R, 2002ApJ...581L..19R], and whose Chandra iron lines motivated the induced-supernova scenario [2001ApJ...555L.117R]
- GRB 130427A / SN 2013cq — prototype Type I BdHN, used to calibrate the Kerr-BH 'inner engine' for GeV emission (M = 2.31 M_sun, spin 0.47, B0 = 3.5x10^10 G) [2019ApJ...886...82R]
- GRB 090510 — the community's flagship short burst, analyzed twice with contradictory classifications [2013ApJ...772...62M, 2016ApJ...831..178R]
- GRB 060614 — interpreted as a 'disguised' short GRB from an old merging binary in a galactic halo (CBM density ~10^-3 cm^-3), explaining the absent supernova [2009A&A...498..501C]
- GRB 090618 — two-episode burst read as a 'proto-black hole' precursor plus a genuine long GRB [2012A&A...543A..10I]
- GRB 090227B — claimed 'missing link' between genuine short and long GRBs [2013ApJ...763..125M]
- The community's own GRB taxonomy: XRFs vs BdHNe among long bursts and S-GRFs vs genuine S-GRBs among short bursts, split at E_iso = 10^52 erg by whether a black hole forms [2016ApJ...832..136R]
- BATSE duration/hardness subpopulations and their brightness distributions, an early statistical strand [1998ApJ...497L..21T]

## Instruments and datasets

- BATSE (burst catalogs, brightness/hardness distributions, GRB 991216 light curves) [1998ApJ...497L..21T, 2001ApJ...555L.113R]
- Rossi X-Ray Timing Explorer and Chandra (GRB 991216 afterglow and iron emission lines) [2001ApJ...555L.113R, 2001ApJ...555L.117R]
- Swift BAT/XRT (light-curve fitting of GRB 060614; the flare-plateau-afterglow sample of BdHNe) [2009A&A...498..501C, 2018ApJ...852...53R]
- Fermi (GBM/LAT) and AGILE for GRB 090510 and the GeV emission of GRB 130427A [2013ApJ...772...62M, 2019ApJ...886...82R]
- Multi-satellite joint spectroscopy — Fermi, Swift, Konus-WIND, AGILE, RT-2, Suzaku — for GRB 090618 [2012A&A...543A..10I]

## Characteristic methods

- General-relativistic and special-relativistic hydrodynamic integration of expanding e+e- pair plasmas, with the validated constant-thickness slab approximation [1999A&A...350..334R, 2000A&A...359..855R, 1999A&AS..138..511R]
- Two-parameter fireshell fitting: total e+e- plasma energy plus baryon load B, with the circumburst-medium density profile reconstructed from the light curve [2009A&A...498..501C, 2013ApJ...772...62M]
- Identification of the P-GRB (transparency emission) versus 'extended afterglow' in prompt light curves, and paradigm-based reinterpretation of burst structure [2001ApJ...555L.113R, 2012A&A...543A..10I]
- Exact equitemporal-surface computation from Taub's relativistic hydrodynamics, contrasted with the approximate EQTS of Panaitescu & Meszaros, Sari, and Granot [2004ApJ...605L...1B]
- Analytical Bondi-Hoyle hypercritical-accretion calculations and full numerical/SPH simulations of SN ejecta accreting onto a NS companion [2012ApJ...758L...7R, 2014ApJ...793L..36F]
- Kerr black-hole electrodynamics in a uniform aligned magnetic field ('inner engine') with synchrotron modeling of the GeV component [2019ApJ...886...82R]
- Statistical brightness-distribution (Euclidean vs non-Euclidean) tests on BATSE duration/hardness subclasses [1998ApJ...497L..21T]

## Recurring models

- Dyadosphere / electromagnetic black hole (EMBH): vacuum polarization above the Heisenberg-Euler critical field around a Reissner-Nordstrom BH as the GRB energy source [1998A&A...338L..87P, 1999A&A...350..334R]
- PEM pulse / fireshell: an optically thick e+e--photon plasma shell that accelerates, engulfs a baryonic remnant, and emits the P-GRB at transparency, followed by 'extended afterglow' from CBM collisions [2000A&A...359..855R, 2001A&A...368..377B, 2009A&A...498..501C]
- The interpretation-of-burst-structure and relative-spacetime-transformation paradigms (injector phase vs beam-target phase; four time variables) [2001ApJ...555L.113R, 2001ApJ...555L.107R]
- Induced gravitational collapse / binary-driven hypernova: SN of a CO core drives hypercritical accretion onto a NS companion that collapses to a BH in ~10^2-10^3 s, emitting the GRB [2012ApJ...758L...7R, 2014ApJ...793L..36F]
- The seven-subclass BdHN taxonomy with the E_iso = 10^52 erg black-hole-formation threshold and GeV emission as the BH discriminant [2016ApJ...832..136R, 2016ApJ...831..178R]
- 'Disguised' short GRBs: long-duration events from old compact binaries in low-density halo environments [2009A&A...498..501C, 2010-era companions]
- The Kerr-BH 'inner engine' operating in elementary ~10^37 erg impulses to produce GeV/TeV emission and UHECRs [2019ApJ...886...82R]

## Results that shaped it

- Definition of the dyadosphere and the demonstration that its pair-creation energetics can meet GRB energy requirements, including GRB 971214 at z=3.4 [1998A&A...338L..87P]
- Validation of the constant-thickness PEM-pulse model against full GR hydrodynamics, yielding Lorentz factors up to 6x10^3 and photon energies 10^52-10^54 erg from EMBH parameters alone [1999A&A...350..334R, 2000A&A...359..855R]
- The GRB 991216 trilogy fixing the model's two free parameters from the afterglow and recasting the 'prompt emission' as afterglow-peak substructure from ISM inhomogeneities at gamma ~ 310, declaring the external-shock model the winner over internal shocks [2001ApJ...555L.113R, 2002ApJ...581L..19R]
- The FRED-shaped elementary spike computed from a pure e+e- PEM pulse without fitting [2001A&A...368..377B]
- Exact equitemporal surfaces differing from the standard approximate ones used in afterglow modeling [2004ApJ...605L...1B]
- The IGC result that a NS companion accreting Type Ib/c SN ejecta reaches critical mass in seconds and collapses to a BH, explaining GRB-SN temporal coincidence and the exclusive association with helium-poor SNe Ic [2012ApJ...758L...7R, 2014ApJ...793L..36F]
- The four/seven-subclass classification of GRBs with the 10^52 erg BH-formation threshold [2016ApJ...832..136R]
- X-ray flares in BdHNe shown to be thermal emission at ~10^12 cm with Gamma <= 4, argued to be incompatible with a single ultrarelativistic fireball jet [2018ApJ...852...53R]
- The self-consistent Kerr-BH inner-engine parameters for GRB 130427A (M = 2.31 M_sun, alpha = 0.47, B0 = 3.48x10^10 G) as the origin of its GeV emission [2019ApJ...886...82R]

## Internal disagreements

- GRB 090510 was classified in two mutually exclusive ways within the same framework: first as a 'disguised short GRB by excess' — an event in an overdense medium (n_CBM ~ 1.85x10^3 cm^-3) defining a new burst family [2013ApJ...772...62M] — and three years later as a 'genuine short GRB' (S-GRB) from a binary neutron star coalescing into a Kerr-Newman black hole, with GeV emission as the BH-formation discriminant [2016ApJ...831..178R]. The two papers assign the same event to different progenitors and different environment classes.
- The direction of causation in the GRB-supernova connection was reversed within the community. The 2001 induced-supernova paradigm had the GRB come first: the progenitor P1 collapses to an EMBH and its burst impacts a nearby companion star P2, inducing the supernova whose remnant produces the iron lines 18.5 hr later [2001ApJ...555L.117R]. The 2012 induced-gravitational-collapse paradigm inverts this: the Type Ib/c supernova explodes first and its ejecta, accreting hypercritically onto the NS companion, induce the collapse to a BH that emits the GRB [2012ApJ...758L...7R]. Both are called 'induced' collapse/explosion, but the trigger and the triggered object are swapped.

## Activity

Active continuously from the 1998-2002 dyadosphere/PEM-pulse founding burst [1998A&A...338L..87P, 2000A&A...359..855R, 2001ApJ...555L.113R] through a per-burst fireshell-fitting phase around 2007-2013 [2009A&A...498..501C, 2012A&A...543A..10I, 2013ApJ...772...62M], peaking around the median year 2015 with the IGC/BdHN reformation [2012ApJ...758L...7R, 2014ApJ...793L..36F, 2016ApJ...832..136R]; still producing new machinery (X-ray flare analysis, inner-engine GeV modeling, afterglow morphology) through 2018-2021 [2018ApJ...852...53R, 2019ApJ...886...82R, 2021MNRAS.504.5301R], so the community remains active at the corpus edge, now centered on the BdHN taxonomy rather than the original dyadosphere.
