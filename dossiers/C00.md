# C0: Classical GRB observations and distance-scale era

**Defining problem.** What are gamma-ray bursts and how far away are they? From the Vela discovery of sixteen 0.2-1.5 MeV bursts of cosmic origin [1973ApJ...182L..85K], this community pursued the phenomenology of the bursts — time structure, energy spectra, sky positions, logN-logS statistics — with the distance scale as the organizing question. KONUS statistics of 85 bursts were read as "proof of a galactic localization" with neutron-star sources [1981Ap&SS..75...47M], while BATSE's isotropic yet inhomogeneous distribution of 153 bursts was "inconsistent with any known population of galactic objects" and pointed to cosmological distances [1992Natur.355..143M], culminating in the 1995 75th-anniversary debate between the cosmological [1995PASP..107.1167P] and Galactic-corona [1995PASP..107.1152L] positions.

## Populations and events

- Classical cosmic gamma-ray bursts detected by Vela satellites, 1969-1972 [1973ApJ...182L..85K]
- The 1979 March 5 event from a flaring X-ray pulsar in Dorado (later SGR 0526-66), repeating on March 6 [1979Natur.282..587M; 1995PASP..107.1152L]
- KONUS catalog bursts from Venera 11/12, Sept 1978 - May 1979 (85 events; several types differing in profile, duration, spectrum; repeating short-burst sources) [1981Ap&SS..80....3M; 1981Ap&SS..75...47M]
- BATSE burst sample: 153 events in the first isotropy analysis, over 1000 by 1995 [1992Natur.355..143M; 1995PASP..107.1167P]
- Short (<2 s, harder) and long (>2 s, softer) burst classes from the first BATSE catalog [1993ApJ...413L.101K]
- Bursts with reported cyclotron/annihilation spectral lines (Venera, Ginga, ISEE 3) [1981Natur.290..378M; 1988Natur.335..234M; 1980ApJ...236L..67T]

## Instruments and datasets

- Vela satellites (Los Alamos gamma-ray detectors) [1973ApJ...182L..85K; 1973ApJ...185L..57W]
- IMP-6 and IMP-7 spectrometers [1973ApJ...185L...1C; 1975ApJ...196L..43C]
- OSO-7 UCSD X-ray telescopes and Apollo 16 gamma/X-ray spectrometers [1973ApJ...185L..57W; 1974ApJ...194L..19M]
- KONUS and Cone experiments on Venera 11/12 [1979Natur.282..587M; 1979SvAL....5...87M; 1981Ap&SS..80....3M]
- ISEE 3 radiatively cooled germanium spectrometer [1980ApJ...236L..67T]
- Ginga burst spectra [1988Natur.335..234M]
- BATSE on the Compton Gamma Ray Observatory: Large Area Detectors for catalogs/statistics, Spectroscopy Detectors for line searches [1992Natur.355..143M; 1993ApJ...413L.101K; 1994ApJ...433L..77P]

## Characteristic methods

- Multi-spacecraft coincidence detection and triangulation of burst arrival directions [1973ApJ...182L..85K; 1973ApJ...185L..57W; 1975ApJ...196L..43C]
- Time-profile and spectral-evolution analysis over keV-MeV bands [1974ApJ...194L..19M; 1973ApJ...185L...1C; 1981Ap&SS..80....3M]
- logN-logS (number vs. intensity) statistics tested against the -3/2 Euclidean-homogeneous law, plus V/Vmax [1979SvAL....5...87M; 1992Natur.355..143M; 1995MNRAS.273..755P]
- Angular (dipole/quadrupole) isotropy tests of burst sky positions [1992Natur.355..143M; 1995PASP..107.1167P; 1995MNRAS.273..755P]
- Spectral-line searches (visual and Bayesian) in burst spectra [1980ApJ...236L..67T; 1981Natur.290..378M; 1994ApJ...433L..77P]
- Duration-distribution and hardness-ratio classification [1993ApJ...413L.101K]
- Monte Carlo population modeling of Galactic halo/corona source distributions [1995MNRAS.273..755P]

## Recurring models

- Galactic neutron-star models: strongly magnetized neutron stars evidenced by cyclotron/annihilation lines [1981Natur.290..378M], accreting neutron-star binaries with non-stationary accretion or thermonuclear fusion [1981Ap&SS..75...47M]
- High-velocity neutron stars in an extended Galactic halo/corona reproducing BATSE isotropy, requiring >600-700 km/s kicks, >10^9 yr active lifetimes, and possibly magnetar fields ~10^15 G [1995MNRAS.273..755P; 1995PASP..107.1152L]
- Cosmological sources at z~1 with ~10^51 erg isotropic energies, e.g. decay of a black-hole/neutron-star binary [1991AcA....41..257P]
- Characteristic empirical spectral form: ~150 keV exponential plus power-law tail, common to all bursts [1973ApJ...185L...1C; 1975ApJ...196L..43C]

## Results that shaped it

- Discovery of sixteen cosmic gamma-ray bursts with Vela, eliminating the Earth and Sun as sources [1973ApJ...182L..85K]
- Confirmation of the hard-spectrum nature of bursts with peak energy release at several hundred keV and a common exponential-plus-power-law spectrum [1973ApJ...185L...1C; 1975ApJ...196L..43C]
- The 1979 March 5 super-burst and its recurrence, the first observational link between bursts and neutron stars [1979Natur.282..587M; 1981Ap&SS..75...47M]
- KONUS catalogs of burst time structure, spectra, and source coordinates, and the claim of galactic localization from logN-logS and anisotropy [1981Ap&SS..80....3M; 1981Ap&SS..75...47M]
- Reported cyclotron and annihilation lines as evidence for strongly magnetized neutron stars [1981Natur.290..378M; 1988Natur.335..234M; 1980ApJ...236L..67T]
- BATSE's isotropic-but-inhomogeneous distribution of 153 bursts, inconsistent with any known galactic population [1992Natur.355..143M]
- Identification of the short/long burst dichotomy at 2 s with duration-hardness anticorrelation [1993ApJ...413L.101K]
- BATSE Spectroscopy Detector non-detection of narrow lines in 192 bursts, undermining the cyclotron-line evidence [1994ApJ...433L..77P]

## Internal disagreements

- Galactic vs. cosmological distance scale: KONUS analyses claimed proof of galactic localization from logN-logS and source anisotropy [1981Ap&SS..75...47M], whereas BATSE found isotropy plus a non -3/2 logN-logS inconsistent with any known galactic population and suggestive of cosmological distances [1992Natur.355..143M]; the dispute was formalized in the 1995 debate, Paczynski arguing the cosmological case [1995PASP..107.1167P; 1991AcA....41..257P] against Lamb's Galactic corona of high-velocity neutron stars anchored on the 1979 March 5 event at 50 kpc [1995PASP..107.1152L], with halo modelers showing non-spherical halos could still mimic BATSE isotropy at the cost of >600-700 km/s kicks and >10^9 yr lifetimes [1995MNRAS.273..755P]
- Reality of spectral lines: Venera/KONUS reported cyclotron and annihilation lines as a characteristic rather than exceptional feature and the most convincing evidence for magnetized neutron stars [1981Natur.290..378M], supported by Ginga cyclotron absorption features [1988Natur.335..234M] and ISEE 3 features at ~420 and ~740 keV [1980ApJ...236L..67T]; but early IMP-6 spectra showed no evidence of line structure [1973ApJ...185L...1C], and BATSE found no convincing narrow lines in 192 bursts, in explicit contrast to Ginga expectations (consistency only at the 13% level) [1994ApJ...433L..77P]
- Interpretation of the logN-logS deficit: the same departure from the -3/2 law seen by KONUS/Cone [1979SvAL....5...87M] was read as evidence for a bounded galactic disk population [1981Ap&SS..75...47M] and later as evidence for a cosmological population with the observer at the center of a bounded spherical distribution [1992Natur.355..143M; 1995PASP..107.1167P]

## Activity

Founded with the 1973 Vela discovery [1973ApJ...182L..85K], with a first observational wave in 1973-1975 (IMP, OSO-7, Apollo) [1973ApJ...185L..57W; 1974ApJ...194L..19M; 1975ApJ...196L..43C], a second in 1979-1981 (Venera/KONUS, ISEE 3) [1979Natur.282..587M; 1981Ap&SS..80....3M; 1980ApJ...236L..67T], and its peak in the BATSE era of the early-to-mid 1990s (median year 1995) around the distance-scale debate [1992Natur.355..143M; 1995PASP..107.1167P; 1995PASP..107.1152L]. The community is essentially historical: its central question was settled for the cosmological scale, with galactic halo models retrospectively noted as ruled out by afterglow and host-galaxy observations [1995MNRAS.273..755P].
