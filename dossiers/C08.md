# C8: GRB Lorentz Invariance Violation Tests

**Defining problem.** Whether quantum gravity leaves an observable imprint on photon propagation over cosmological distances — specifically an energy-dependent speed of light (vacuum dispersion) or vacuum birefringence suppressed by the Planck scale — and whether gamma-ray bursts, by combining GeV–TeV photon energies, millisecond variability, and known redshifts, can push time-of-flight and polarization constraints to and beyond E_Planck ~ 1.22e19 GeV. The program was founded by the proposal that GRB arrival-time differences across energy bands are sensitive to Planck-scale dispersion [1998Natur.393..763A], theoretically grounded in loop-quantum-gravity predictions of nonstandard vacuum optics [1999PhRvD..59l4021G], and its persistent core difficulty is the degeneracy between propagation-induced delays and intrinsic source spectral lags [2006APh....25..402E, 2000ApJ...535..139E, 2004ApJ...611L..77B].

## Populations and events

- GRB 090510 (short, distant; 31 GeV photon; the flagship single-event Planck-scale limit) [2009Natur.462..331A]
- GRB 221009A (the BOAT; tens of thousands of LHAASO TeV photons, z~0.151) [2024PhRvD.109h1501P, 2023JPhG...50fLT01Z]
- GRB 021206 (claimed 80±20% polarized prompt emission, later retracted) [2003Natur.423..415C, 2004MNRAS.350.1288R, 2004PhRvL..93b1101J, 2004ApJ...611L..77B]
- GRB 160509A and GRB 160625B (claimed light-speed-variation signals and spectral-lag transition) [2016PhLB..760..602X, 2017APh....94...17G]
- BATSE-era GRBs with redshifts: GRB 930229, GRB 980703, GRB 930131 microsecond-rise-time flares [1999PhRvL..82.4964S]; optically identified BATSE/OSSE GRB samples [2000ApJ...535..139E, 2003A&A...402..409E]
- Mkn 501 AGN flare observed by MAGIC (2.5-sigma energy-dependent delay hint) [2008PhLB..668..253M]
- Crab Nebula synchrotron electrons (vacuum-Cherenkov bound) [2004PhRvL..93b1101J]
- Predicted neutrino bursts accompanying GRBs (~1e5 GeV) [2000PhRvL..84.2318A]

## Instruments and datasets

- CGRO BATSE (64 ms and TTE data) and OSSE light curves of redshift-identified GRBs [2000ApJ...535..139E, 2003A&A...402..409E]
- Fermi-LAT high-energy photons, including Pass 8 data of bright GRBs [2009Natur.462..331A, 2015APh....61..108Z, 2023JPhG...50fLT01Z]
- Fermi-GBM and HEBS keV-MeV light curves as timing anchors [2023JPhG...50fLT01Z]
- LHAASO 0.2-7 TeV photon data on GRB 221009A [2024PhRvD.109h1501P]
- MAGIC imaging atmospheric Cherenkov telescope [2008PhLB..668..253M]
- RHESSI Compton-scattering polarimetry [2003Natur.423..415C, 2004MNRAS.350.1288R]
- Optical/UV linear polarization spectra of distant sources [2001PhRvD..64h3007G]

## Characteristic methods

- Time-of-flight / arrival-time analysis of photons in different energy bands, with the delay parametrized as Delta-t proportional to E/M (linear) or E^2/M^2 (quadratic) [1998Natur.393..763A, 2004ApJ...611L..77B, 2024PhRvD.109h1501P]
- Regression of energy-dependent lags against redshift over GRB samples to separate propagation (distance-dependent) from source (intrinsic) effects [2000ApJ...535..139E, 2006APh....25..402E]
- Wavelet shrinkage and wavelet-zoom identification of sharp light-curve features for precise timing [2003A&A...402..409E, 2006APh....25..402E]
- Single-event limits from microsecond rise times and simultaneous multi-band arrival [1999PhRvL..82.4964S, 2009Natur.462..331A]
- Vacuum-birefringence constraints from linear polarization of distant sources and of prompt GRB emission [2001PhRvD..64h3007G, 2004PhRvL..93b1101J]
- Effective-field-theory parametrization of Planck-suppressed Lorentz-violating operators, plus vacuum-Cherenkov threshold arguments [2004PhRvL..93b1101J]
- Monte Carlo noise simulations to validate feature detection and instrument sensitivity [2006APh....25..402E, 2008PhLB..668..253M]
- Frequentist and information-criterion (AIC/BIC) model comparison between LIV and intrinsic-lag models [2017APh....94...17G]

## Recurring models

- Energy-dependent vacuum refractive index c(E) = c0(1 - E/M) from quantum-gravitational 'space-time foam' [2000ApJ...535..139E, 2006APh....25..402E, 2008PhLB..668..253M]
- Loop quantum gravity semiclassical states producing helicity-dependent (birefringent) corrections to Maxwell's equations — the Gambini-Pullin effect [1999PhRvD..59l4021G, 2001PhRvD..64h3007G]
- Linear (n=1) vs quadratic (n=2) energy dependence of the dispersion, mapped onto Standard-Model-Extension operator dimensions [2003A&A...402..409E, 2024PhRvD.109h1501P]
- Effective field theory with Planck-suppressed Lorentz-violating operators for photons and electrons [2004PhRvL..93b1101J]
- Loop-quantum-gravity corrections to spin-1/2 (neutrino) propagation and oscillation lengths [2000PhRvL..84.2318A]
- Intrinsic-source spectral-lag models as the systematic that any LIV claim must beat [2006APh....25..402E, 2017APh....94...17G]

## Results that shaped it

- Amelino-Camelia et al. showed GRB timing is sensitive to dispersion at the Planck scale (~1e19 GeV), founding the field [1998Natur.393..763A]
- Gambini & Pullin derived helicity-dependent corrections to Maxwell's equations from loop quantum gravity, giving the program a concrete theoretical target [1999PhRvD..59l4021G]
- Schaefer set early severe limits: Delta-c/c < 6.3e-21, m_gamma < 4.2e-44 g, E_QG > 8.3e16 GeV from BATSE GRB flares [1999PhRvL..82.4964S]
- Ellis et al. introduced the redshift-regression method on BATSE/OSSE GRBs, obtaining M > ~1e15 GeV [2000ApJ...535..139E], refined with wavelet timing to M >= 6.9e15 GeV [2003A&A...402..409E], and then to a robust M > 0.9e16 GeV while finding a systematic tendency for energetic photons to arrive earlier that is degenerate with intrinsic lags [2006APh....25..402E]
- Jacobson, Liberati & Mattingly used the (since-disputed) GRB 021206 polarization plus Crab vacuum-Cherenkov arguments to improve EFT Lorentz-violation bounds by 10 orders of magnitude [2004PhRvL..93b1101J]
- MAGIC found a 2.5-sigma energy-dependent delay in a Mkn 501 flare, the community's most discussed positive hint outside GRBs [2008PhLB..668..253M]
- Fermi-LAT's GRB 090510 gave the watershed single-event limit M > 1.2 E_Planck for linear dispersion, disfavoring linear quantum-gravity models [2009Natur.462..331A]
- LHAASO TeV photons from GRB 221009A pushed limits to >5.9 E_Planck (linear) and the best quadratic time-of-flight limits [2024PhRvD.109h1501P]

## Internal disagreements

- Positive light-speed-variation claims vs Planck-scale null limits: Zhang & Ma report that Fermi-LAT Pass 8 bright-GRB events support an energy-dependent light speed with linear and quadratic corrections [2015APh....61..108Z], Xu & Ma find evidence for a linear light-speed modification from GRB 160509A [2016PhLB..760..602X], and Zhu & Ma argue the 99.3 GeV photon of GRB 221009A is consistent with E_LV = 3.6e17 GeV [2023JPhG...50fLT01Z]. This is directly contradicted by the GRB 090510 limit M > 1.2 E_Planck ~ 1.5e19 GeV [2009Natur.462..331A] and by LHAASO GRB 221009A limits > 5.9 E_Planck [2024PhRvD.109h1501P], which explicitly confirm stringent limits from other GRBs; Ganguly & Desai likewise conclude the GRB 160625B spectral-lag transition, even if real, cannot be LIV because it would violate prior limits by 3-4 orders of magnitude and must be intrinsic [2017APh....94...17G].
- The GRB 021206 polarization retraction: Coburn & Boggs claimed near-maximal (80±20%) linear polarization of the prompt emission from RHESSI data [2003Natur.423..415C], and this measurement underpinned the 1e10-fold improvement in birefringence LIV bounds [2004PhRvL..93b1101J] and Lorentz-invariance tests on the same burst [2004ApJ...611L..77B]; Rutledge & Fox re-analyzed the RHESSI data and found the burst was not 80±20% polarized, that Coburn & Boggs overestimated usable scattering events by a factor of 10, and that the signal-to-noise could not yield a detection even for a 100% polarized source [2004MNRAS.350.1288R].
- Propagation effect vs intrinsic source lags: Ellis et al. found a systematic tendency for more energetic photons to arrive earlier across a large GRB sample, but showed the intrinsic-lag and propagation parameters are strongly degenerate and the significance weakens for robust features, so they published only a lower limit [2006APh....25..402E]; the MAGIC collaboration similarly could not exclude a source effect behind their 2.5-sigma Mkn 501 delay [2008PhLB..668..253M], while the Ma-group papers attribute equivalent time lags to genuine propagation effects [2016PhLB..760..602X, 2023JPhG...50fLT01Z].

## Activity

Founded 1998-2000 by the Amelino-Camelia proposal and loop-quantum-gravity phenomenology [1998Natur.393..763A, 1999PhRvD..59l4021G, 2000PhRvL..84.2318A]; a BATSE-era constraint wave 1999-2006 [1999PhRvL..82.4964S, 2000ApJ...535..139E, 2003A&A...402..409E, 2006APh....25..402E]; reinvigorated by MAGIC and Fermi 2008-2009 [2008PhLB..668..253M, 2009Natur.462..331A]. The median year of 2017 reflects its most active recent phase, the 2015-2018 Ma-group light-speed-variation claims and their rebuttals [2015APh....61..108Z, 2016PhLB..760..602X, 2017APh....94...17G]. It remains active: GRB 221009A/LHAASO produced both a new positive claim [2023JPhG...50fLT01Z] and the strongest-ever time-of-flight limits [2024PhRvD.109h1501P].
